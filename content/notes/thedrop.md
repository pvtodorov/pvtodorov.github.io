# Ripples I'm looking forward to at The Drop 2026

I'm heading to the Drop this year and looking forward to connecting with founders, investors, and other key players in the deeptech ecosystem who focus on planetary health and resilience. Here are some of the roundtables and side-events I found to be interesting (will be a difficult choice to pick which ones to attend!)

Looking forward to run into and speak with others who share the same interests.

- **[Natural Solutions: Valuable or Money Wasted?](https://luma.com/m6jayd88)** · [Jeff Johnson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jeffreyjohnson/) ([B Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/b-capital-group)) and [Kyle Teamey](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kyle-teamey-9a53197) ([RA Capital Planetary Health](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ra-capital-management-llc))

  > "Money to combat climate change is limited and must be spent in the most efficient manner possible."

  "Studies of marginal abatement costs indicate we can abate emissions much more efficiently via building insulation and vehicle emissions programs than via natural solutions." · why are there so few successful examples of businesses leveraging natural solutions · is there a business model, or should this be the domain of philanthropies and governments · "Resources are scarce. In light of this should we make the hard choice to limit or exclude natural solutions?" · for individuals currently investing or contemplating investing time or money in natural solutions

- **[Conventional agriculture: the efficiency myth](https://luma.com/lx7iowcj)** · [Sanne Bootsma](https://nl.linkedin.com/in/sanne-bootsma) ([DOEN Ventures](https://www.linkedin.com/company/doen-participaties/)) and [Erica van Eeghen](https://nl.linkedin.com/in/ericavaneeghen) ([VP Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/vpcapital-familyoffice))

  > "Heavy reliance on fossil-based inputs, degraded soils and inefficient land use are creating growing environmental and economic risks."

  Whether regenerative agriculture can become the economically superior model, and what it takes to realign finance, policy, technology and incentives · soil regeneration cycles of 7 to 10 years against capital cycles of 3 to 5 years · should the healthcare and insurance industries help fund food system resilience · how costs are shared across beneficiaries · core tension: short-term economic efficiency of the agricultural system v long-term ecosystem resilience · for investors, agtech founders, corporate sustainability leads, farmers, policymakers and financial institutions

- **[Food to Fashion to Food: Bio Closed-Loop](https://luma.com/nphtlr7p)** · [Nic Gorini](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nicgorini/) ([Spin Ventures](https://uk.linkedin.com/company/spin-ventures)) and [Michelle de Rijk](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michelle-de-rijk-bba96661/) ([Fair Capital Impact Fund](https://www.linkedin.com/company/fair-capital-impact-fund/))

  > "The unfair advantage of waste to value models and insetting strategies versus offsetting ones."

  Closed-loop materials as an asset class, waste to value via industrial symbiosis, "lower impact 0 toxicity and better returns" · "The EU generates over 58 million tonnes of food waste annually, valued at about €132bn" · "the European textile and clothing sector comprises roughly 192,000 companies, 1.3 million workers, and around €167bn in turnover" · European bioeconomy "already valued at €2.7tn and supporting more than 17 million jobs in 2023" · which Food to Fashion to Food systems deliver venture-scale returns in Europe before 2030 · how feedstock aggregation locks in long-term input cost advantage · which infrastructure drives asset-level returns as textile collection volumes rise under 2025 EU rules · for biotech and deeptech ventures, agri, consumer and retail operators, LPs and GPs

- **[Hardware & Biology: Durable Moats Beyond Code](https://luma.com/gc1tdw4k)** · Max Lebeau ([Positron Ventures](https://www.linkedin.com/company/positron-ventures)) and [Isabel Zhang](https://www.linkedin.com/in/isabel-zhang/) ([HCVC](https://www.linkedin.com/company/hardware-club))

  > "As AI commoditizes software features overnight, hardware and biological systems offer structural defensibility that code cannot match."

  How founders and investors build durable advantage while navigating capital intensity, regulatory complexity and physical constraints · "why the next decade of defensibility lives in atoms and cells, not algorithms" · for hardware and biotech founders, software people looking at deeptech, and investors refining their thesis

- **[Food investment - the next wave (the Tsunami)](https://luma.com/thedrop-buu0)** · [Johan Jörgensen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/johanjorgensen/) ([Sweden Foodtech](https://www.linkedin.com/company/swedenfoodtech)) and [Björn Öste](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bj%C3%B6rn-%C3%B6ste-b185551/) (Good Idea)

  > "Food will be bigger than AI - remember where you heard it first!"

  "Rethinking food is the most urgent topic there is - it's the major source of climate change, destroyed nature and bad human health." · "The dry and flaming summer of -26 has, together with the GLP-1 revolution, driven home the message." · we actually don't know anything about food and humans, and the science is developing at breakneck speed · "we're facing a Copernican shift and a similar transformation" · "It helps if you're somewhat versed in science and open to changing your worldviews." · for investors looking for new hypotheses, food tech investors and entrepreneurs

- **[What Nature Markets Need to Scale](https://luma.com/wttd4uva)** · [Ed Thorne](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ethorne1/) ([Sand River](https://uk.linkedin.com/company/sand-river)) with Kevin Webb

  > "The clean energy transition didn't happen because of wind farms", "it happened because of batteries, turbines and PV cells."

  Nature markets in 2026 compared to clean energy in the early 2010s · companies building supply chain transparency, biological inputs that compete economically with synthetics, tradeable nature assets, transition risk assessment tools · which bottlenecks are blocking institutional capital and who is tackling them first · whether regenerative technologies can outcompete extractive alternatives on economics alone · what the nature markets landscape looks like by 2030 · for investors, founders, students, policymakers and capital stewards

- **[Ocean tech: too early or mis-funded?](https://luma.com/5b5vg4tm)** · [Jerome Ternynck](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jerometernynck/) ([Rypples](https://www.linkedin.com/company/rypples/)) and [James Lindsay](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jamesarthurlindsay/) ([Builders Vision](https://www.linkedin.com/company/builders-vision))

  > "This isn't an intro session, it's a debate for people ready to take a position."

  Ocean tech "has been on the periphery of climate investing for years despite its massive potential" · ocean ecosystems, "one of the biggest buffers we have", remain chronically underfunded · the gap between what the science demands and where capital flows "is starting to look less like caution and more like a mis-pricing we can't afford" · too early for venture, or measured with the wrong metrics · where the bottleneck sits, in technology readiness, patient capital or investor familiarity · which sub-sectors are closest to breakout · "Capital efficiency vs. planetary necessity" · for climate and deep tech investors "ocean-curious but haven't yet made a bet", plus adjacent verticals

- **[Can Robots secure Europe's Food System?](https://luma.com/8yda2rik)** · [Julius Strauss](https://www.linkedin.com/in/julius-strauss/) ([FoodLabs](https://www.linkedin.com/company/foodlabsvc/)) and [Philip Stark](https://www.linkedin.com/in/philipstark/) ([Oyster Bay](https://www.linkedin.com/company/oysterbayvc/))

  > "Hormuz, Ukraine, AGI - threats to food supply are increasing, and Europe needs to make more of what we eat. But with labour costs rising and immigration rules tightening, can robots ride to the rescue?"

  "Rising labour costs, rising input costs, rising transport costs; but robots are getting dramatically smarter and cheaper" · which are the best opportunities, the most urgent shortages and the most robotics-ready · what makes robotics startups uniquely hard, and the keys to winning · how Europe competes with China and the US · "Food sovereignty is never a priority until it's too late." · for founders and investors in ag robotics, and anyone interested in food security or dual-use defence applications

- **[Is Resilience a Lie We Tell Ourselves?](https://luma.com/070moc9e)** · [Till Stenzel](https://www.linkedin.com/in/drtillstenzel/) ([SET Ventures](https://www.linkedin.com/company/setventures)) and [Katharina Beitz](https://www.linkedin.com/in/thedigitalkatharinabeitz/) ([Vireo Ventures](https://www.linkedin.com/company/vireo-ventures))

  > "Decentralization removes the honeypot but creates millions of entry points."

  Removing single points of failure while "replacing them with millions of new attack vectors" · "The planned cyberattack on distributed energy assets in Poland in late 2025" · "The gap between deployment speed and security readiness is widening - not closing." · most energy tech investors still don't look at cybersecurity when evaluating deals · "where is the line between civilian infrastructure and strategic assets" · are we building resilience or "just trading one set of risks for another" · for operators of distributed energy assets, energy tech investors and founders

- **[Mine or Be Mined](https://luma.com/vjr3v0xh)** · [Lasse Köhler](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lassekohler/) ([EIFO](https://www.linkedin.com/company/eifo)) and [Johan Attby](https://www.linkedin.com/in/johanattby/) ([Norrsken Evolve](https://www.linkedin.com/company/norrsken-evolve))

  > "Europe faces a strategic choice."

  Strengthen domestic extraction and processing, deepen partnerships with trusted allies, or accelerate investment in technologies that reduce reliance on scarce materials altogether · recent export controls on rare earth elements · implementation of the EU's Critical Raw Materials Act · extraction, processing and refining are "capital-intensive, complex, and subject to long development cycles, making them a challenging fit for traditional venture capital" · what role sovereign wealth and philanthropic capital should play alongside VC · "decisions made over the coming years will likely shape the balance of industrial competitiveness for decades" · for climate, industrial, defence and energy transition investors, battery and advanced materials founders, corporates with supply chain exposure and policymakers

- **[European Sovereignty Playbook](https://luma.com/bg7zzwf9)** · [Maxi Pethö-Schramm](https://de.linkedin.com/in/anneke-maxi-peth%C3%B6-schramm) ([HV Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/hvcapital)) and [Birthe Ross](https://de.linkedin.com/in/birthe-ross/de) ([SPRIND](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sprind/))

  > "A new generation of European companies is emerging across energy, critical minerals, semiconductors, robotics, defence, and compute."

  Building "European primes", companies whose significance exceeds traditional venture outcomes · the right capitalisation structures for companies destined to be critical national or continental assets · building government relationships, winning contracts, navigating policy · foreign private capital or European public funding · European sovereignty v global integration · government process v startup speed · sovereignty still defined and funded nationally · for founders in dual-use technology, energy and industrial sectors, public sector operators, sovereign investors and policymakers

- **[Climate Risk & the Insurability Crisis](https://luma.com/enpf9hbi)** · [Laura Fox](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lauraafox/) ([Streetlife Ventures](https://www.linkedin.com/company/streetlifevc)) and [Michael Langguth](https://www.linkedin.com/in/langguthmichael/) ([Carbon13](https://uk.linkedin.com/company/carbon13))

  > "25% of homes are at risk of uninsurability (and rising), and homes represent the greatest single share (40%) of household savings."

  Over $12 trillion in US home values projected at risk over the next 30 years · over €800 billion in European climate-related losses since 1980 · only about 25% of losses in Europe covered · nearly 40 million homes facing reduced insurance availability · two hurricanes in North Carolina, $80 billion in damages · which sectors face the most pressing insurability challenges · which approaches are economically viable and scalable · tension: significant changes to the built environment v unlivable areas · for investors, founders, corporates and family offices holding real estate, energy and transport assets, plus insurance professionals

- **[Unlocking Hidden Innovation](https://luma.com/wuvj8a8s)** · [Andrea Siciliani](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andreasiciliani/) ([Techenergy Ventures](https://www.linkedin.com/company/techenergyventures)) and [Leonardo Massa](https://it.linkedin.com/in/leonardomassa) ([MITO Technology](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mito-technology))

- **[De-risking the Dragon, embracing the Elephant](https://luma.com/8mf952hr)** · [Divya Murthy](https://de.linkedin.com/in/divyabn) ([Oyster Bay](https://www.linkedin.com/company/oysterbayvc/)) and [Mark Kahn](https://ae.linkedin.com/in/mark-kahn-20490a) ([Omnivore](https://in.linkedin.com/company/omnivore-partners))

- **[Infra-tech: New definitions & funding modes](https://luma.com/53sqyovx)** · [Shakil Karim](https://www.linkedin.com/in/shakil-karim-5080b412a/) ([Actia Capital Partners](https://www.linkedin.com/company/actia-partners)) and [Toba Spiegel](https://at.linkedin.com/in/tobaspiegel) ([Loom Ventures](https://at.linkedin.com/company/loomvc))
